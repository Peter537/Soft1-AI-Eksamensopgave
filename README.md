## Business case / Problem statement

Across the world, cars are one of the most essential modes of transportation. In some regions more than others, which leads to a high volume of vehicle transactions. For example, according to S&P Global:

&emsp;&emsp;_"[...] 2025 US auto sales in April expected to reach 1.49 million units [...]"_  
  Source: [automotive-insights](https://www.spglobal.com/automotive-insights/en/blogs/2025/02/us-auto-sales-2025) (last visited: 29-04-2025).

Where there is a high number of cars being bought and sold, they will eventually end up at used car dealerships. According to [IBISWorld](https://www.ibisworld.com/united-states/number-of-businesses/used-car-dealers/1004/) (last visited: 29-04-2025): There were

&emsp;&emsp;_"There was 149,002 Used Car Dealers in the US businesses as of 2024, an decrease of -1.7% from 2023."_

Based on this, we can wonder:

- How can these used car dealerships price their cars?
- What factors impact the pricing of a used car?
- Can machine learning be used to provide an accurate price prediction?
- Can a Chatbot accurately answer user questions for the car dealerships?

### Hypothesis

We believe that we can create a machine learning model that can predict the price of a used car based on its attributes. We also believe that we can create a chatbot that can answer user questions about the car dealership and its cars based on the dataset from part1.
